Limitations & Future Work

The system detects diabetic retinopathy reliably — and grades its severity unreliably, especially at the severe end.

What limits it

  • One dataset, one region, a handful of camerasNothing here shows it would transfer elsewhere
  • One grader per image; 2.1 % of labels contradict themselvesNo model can exceed its labels
  • No patient identifiers in APTOSThe same eye could sit in two splits
  • Severe has only 26 test imagesIts F1 could be 0.11 or 0.47 — too wide to call
  • It under-grades severe disease14 of 26 Severe eyes were called Moderate

What would come next

  • Test transfer on EyePACS or IDRiDThe largest unknown
  • Combine class weights with EfficientNetB3Weights gave the highest sensitivity — never tried together
  • Train a second seed for B3One 17-minute run closes that gap
  • Use an ordinal, stage-aware loss75 % of errors are one stage away
  • Add an “image not gradable” outputLarge errors cluster in the blurriest third
Before clinical use: prospective validation, several populations and cameras, regulatory approval, and a defined role for the clinician.
